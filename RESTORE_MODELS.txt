FarmPilot 3.0.3 includes the source, prebuilt offline app, English feedback model and runtime. Optional translation weights are separate release assets: FarmPilot_Models_Swahili.zip, FarmPilot_Models_French.zip and FarmPilot_Models_Spanish.zip. Import a ZIP through the web app, or extract its FarmPilot/public contents into this checkout before building a complete multilingual desktop installer. No retraining is needed. See docs/PUBLISH.md.
